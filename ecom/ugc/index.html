How is the content made?+

Through our own creator-style production pipeline. On the strategy call we walk you through exactly how it works for your product and what we need from you.

Do we need to send products?+

It depends on the product and the approach. Sometimes photos are enough, sometimes a sample helps. We'll confirm on the strategy call.

Can we run these as paid ads?+

Yes. UGC is delivered ad-ready in vertical and square formats, with hook variations for testing.

How many videos should we start with?+

Most brands start with a small batch to test hooks and angles, then scale what works into a monthly flow.

Which products work best?+

Fashion, beauty, food and lifestyle products: anything people wear, use or taste on camera.
