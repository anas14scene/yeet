Case studies

The brief. The ad.The outcome.

Three products, three categories, one goal: make people want it before they've finished scrolling.

Case 01 · Beverage

FURY

Challenge
Make a new energy drink look as bold as the category leaders from the very first ad.
Execution
A dark, high-contrast hero film. Ice, a slow-motion pour and the gold Fury can and bottle, lit like a premium spirit.
Result
A launch-ready hero spot plus cutdowns for Reels, TikTok and paid social, all built around one ownable look.

Case 02 · Fragrance

bubblz

Challenge
Make a scent visible. Sell a salted-caramel fragrance mist to shoppers who can't smell it through a screen.
Execution
A soft, peach-toned product film with floating bubbles, caramel textures and macro detail that turn the fragrance into pictures.
Result
An editorial-grade asset that makes the product feel premium and gives the brand a visual signature people recognise.

Case 03 · Food

EL DOHA

Challenge
Make an everyday pantry product, Easy Pasta, feel indulgent enough to stop the scroll.
Execution
Steaming, cheesy macaroni, jalapeño close-ups and a floating pack shot, cut for appetite from the first frame.
Result
Crave-led content that works across social ads, retail screens and delivery-app listings.
