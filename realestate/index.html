Case studies

The brief. The film.The outcome.

Three projects, three problems, one approach: show the finished result before anyone has to imagine it.

Case 01 · Developments

AEON TOWERS

Challenge
Market a multi-tower residential project to buyers while it was still a construction site.
Execution
A vertical construction-to-completion film: cranes and foundations rise into finished, illuminated towers in one continuous shot.
Result
One hero asset that works on launch-event screens, in the sales centre and in paid social, showing buyers the finished product today.

Case 02 · Interior Design

the design bureau

Challenge
Show prospective clients the studio's finished design language, not a room full of tools and bare plaster.
Execution
A shell-to-finish transformation. Workers, raw floors and bare walls dissolve into a fully styled, warmly lit living space.
Result
A portfolio piece that sells the studio's process and its outcome in seconds, ready for Instagram, pitches and the website.

Case 03 · Hospitality

Pyramid Airbnb

Challenge
Stand out in a crowded short-stay market where every listing claims "the best view".
Execution
A cinematic landscape film that guides guests through the space and lands on the pyramids, framed through the window.
Result
Listing-ready content that turns the property's one-of-a-kind view into the headline, the hook and the reason to book.
