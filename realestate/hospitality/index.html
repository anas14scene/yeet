FAQ

Questions,answered.

Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do you work with single Airbnb units, or only hotels?+

Both. From a single short-stay apartment to hotels, resorts, restaurants and clubs, the approach scales to the property.

Can you market a property that hasn't opened yet?+

Yes. We can work from renders and plans to build pre-opening content, so bookings and buzz start before launch day.

Will the videos work on Airbnb and booking sites?+

We deliver the formats each platform and social channel needs, including vertical, square and landscape.

Do you need to visit the property?+

Not always. Depending on the project, we can work from your existing footage, photos or renders. We'll confirm on the strategy call.

How long does it take?+

It depends on the scope. A single listing film moves faster than a seasonal campaign. We confirm a delivery date on the strategy call, before you commit.
